# Source context

- Frozen accession provenance and extraction annotations: `references/metadata.tsv` and `references/input_manifest.json`.
- MiFish methodological reference: Miya et al. 2015, https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.150088 . Transfer of the conservation/specificity and short-amplicon design approach, not a claim that fish 12S primers work in crinoid 16S.
- Crinoid 16S usage: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12179643/ . Molecular taxonomic study using mitochondrial 16S, COI and nuclear 28S; it does not validate these MiLily primers.
- Mitogenome context: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10478607/ . Poliometra prolixa mitochondrial genome with 16S annotation.
